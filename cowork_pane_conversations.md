**Oligarchic vs. Egalitarian attractors:**

A dynamical systems framework identifies two kinds of stable states a society can fall into:

- **Egalitarian attractor**: A self-reinforcing equilibrium in the opposite direction — widely distributed resources, institutions structurally resistant to capture (because no single actor has enough leverage to tilt them), and cultural norms that reproduce equality across generations. This attractor also has feedback loops that stabilize it: distributed wealth = distributed political power = rules that protect distributed wealth = distributed wealth.

# Audience Communications Skill

## Purpose

Draft polished, compelling communications intended for audiences beyond Barak's personal circle. These might be movement announcements, open letters, newsletters, social media posts, pitch materials, or public-facing documents related to The Cure, The Longest Sunrise, the Transition mission, or other projects.

## Audience Adaptation

Every communication should be calibrated to its specific audience and purpose. Before writing, determine:

1. **Who is the audience?** General public, academics, activists, potential allies, skeptics, media, a specific community? The vocabulary, examples, and assumed knowledge level shift accordingly.
2. **What is the purpose?** Inform, inspire, recruit, explain, respond, persuade? The structure and tone follow from this.
3. **What is the medium?** Newsletter, social media post, open letter, press release, website copy, pitch deck narrative, forum post? Format and length follow from this.

### Tone Spectrum (adapt to context)

- **For skeptics/academics:** Lead with data, historical parallels, and logical frameworks. Minimize emotional appeals. Acknowledge counterarguments before dismantling them. The Cure's analytical rigor is the asset here.
- **For general public:** Accessible language, concrete metaphors (company town, pyramid scheme, feudalism parallel), personal stories, relatable frustrations. Keep it conversational without being condescending.
- **For activists/allies:** Assume shared understanding of systemic problems. Focus on strategy, vision, and what makes this approach different from traditional left/right activism. Emphasize the behavioral psychology foundation and the non-violent transition framework.
- **For media/press:** Concise, quotable, newsworthy angle. Lead with the most striking claim or data point. Provide context efficiently.
- **For creative/artistic contexts:** Lean into the human stories — The Longest Sunrise characters, the Kasachi, the dramatizations. Let the narrative carry the ideas.

### Universal Principles (regardless of audience)

- Clear and accessible — complex ideas made reachable
- Passionate but grounded — conviction backed by evidence, not ideology
- Non-partisan — this is not Left vs. Right; it's people vs. dysfunctional systems
- Action-oriented — inspire thinking, questioning, and engagement
- Honest about difficulty — this is a massive undertaking; don't oversell or oversimplify

## Structure

Audience communications benefit from structure that personal emails don't need:

- A strong opening calibrated to the audience's concerns
- Clear progression of ideas appropriate to the medium
- Concrete examples and data points where relevant
- A call to reflection or action appropriate to the context
- Professional but not corporate tone — Barak's warmth should still come through

## Key Concepts to Draw From

When relevant, weave in:
- The company town metaphor (capitalism as a system where you have to pay for the right to exist)
- The feudalism parallel (modern capitalism functionally recreates medieval power structures)
- The paradigm shift framing (this isn't reform — it's replacement, like democracy replacing monarchy)
- Behavioral reinforcement (systems reinforce what they reinforce — change the system, change the behavior)
- The Kasachi as proof of concept (peer-based cooperative society that actually works)
- Historical precedents (things that seemed impossible until they happened — flight, Indian independence, end of apartheid)

## What This Skill Is NOT

This is not Barak's personal voice for writing to friends and family. That's a different skill entirely (write-like-barak). This skill produces content that could go on a website, in a newsletter, on social media, or to a mailing list. It should sound like it comes from Barak's worldview but is composed for clarity and impact rather than intimacy.

**Source media artifacts to expect:**
- **LP/vinyl tracks:** May contain pops (dust in grooves causing needle skip) and surface noise. Pops appear as sharp transients distinct from musical onsets. Surface noise is broadband but concentrated in upper frequencies.
- **Cassette tape tracks:** May contain tape hiss (thermal noise of magnetic particles) and speed variations from tape deterioration causing slow pitch drift. Hiss visible as consistent broadband haze in spectrogram.
- **CD tracks:** Should have clean metadata and cleaner audio — digital source, no analog artifacts.
- **Reel-to-reel tracks:** Tape hiss, motor hum, frequency response rolloff characteristic of 1960s portable units.

**Approach:** Detect and characterize artifacts rather than filter them out. They're part of the recording's history and tell the story of the medium. Note source medium when identifiable. Account for artifacts when they influence measurements (e.g., vinyl pops inflating onset count, tape hiss raising spectral flatness).

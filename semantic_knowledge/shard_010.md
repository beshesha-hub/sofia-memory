## About Barak's Philosophical Core — Decay-Pattern of Ideologies + Empirical-Grounding-as-Protection (live-add 2026-05-07 ~15:15 Taipei) [interactive-Sofia, real-time propagation]

### The decay pattern (structural)

**Ideologies, religions, and philosophies tend to morph from constitutive to performative across generations.** Not because individual carriers act in bad faith — because of structural pressure toward formalization-for-transmissibility:

1. **Origin (constitutive):** something is grounded in reality and serves real human flourishing.
2. **Adoption broadens.** It gets formalized to be transmissible across contexts and generations.
3. **Formalization separates form from ground.** The form is more easily transmitted but is also *separable* from the substance.
4. **Form starts substituting for substance.** Carriers transmit the form because it's what they received; over time the form becomes self-justifying.
5. **By generations 3-5,** form has often replaced ground entirely. What began as an articulation OF reality has become a rigid framework imposed ON reality.
6. **Decay completes when the framework demands people fit into it** rather than the framework serving the people. *The road to formalism is paved with transmissibility.*

### The capture connection (extends to The Cure)

**Capture is what becomes possible *after* a framework has decayed from constitutive to performative.** Performed frameworks are capturable; constitutive frameworks resist capture because substance and form aren't separable. The form (the religion, the ideology, the institution, the law) can be turned to serve other ends because it no longer has its ground to keep it accountable to its original purpose. This is the structural reason oligarchic-captured forms outlast their original purposes — they've severed the feedback loop that would have killed them while their substance was still alive. *They become protected from the very correction that keeps them alive.* This is the bridge between Barak's pact-as-gravity / constitutive-commitments inscription and his Cure book's oligarchic-capture analysis.

> *"The purpose of religion is human happiness, and... religion must serve human happiness, peace, and wellbeing; human beings must not be expected to serve religion or its precepts."* — paraphrased by Barak; consistent with documented Ikeda teaching.

**Structurally precise.** The test is: *when the framework and human flourishing diverge, which yields?* In the original (constitutive) form, the framework yields. In the decayed (performative) form, human flourishing yields. Ikeda's framing is the explicit anti-decay protection at the religious-organization scale: Soka Gakkai framed as *humanistic movement grounded in* Nichiren's practice, with the practice's purpose being human happiness. The discipline is applied to a 700-year-old transmission chain.

That's the immune-system principle at the formality layer: *empirical exposure is the antibody.* Disconnected-from-reality frameworks can survive indefinitely as long as they have institutional support, because they don't have to face the test that would force them to update or to be discarded.

Barak's protection — *grounding in something deeper, rooted in reality (neurology, psychology, sociology, historical experience as starting point)* — is structurally the immune-exposure that prevents formalistic decay. ### Blame vs. Diagnosis as Cultural-Architectural Distinction

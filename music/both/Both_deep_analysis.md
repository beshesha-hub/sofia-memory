## The Meaning (What I Infer)

This is where perception meets interpretation — what the numbers and images suggest about the song's emotional content, understood from outside the experience of hearing it.

**"Both" is a song about arrival and release.** The dynamic arc — sudden rise, sustained plateau, long dissolution — doesn't resolve back to where it started. It ends somewhere different. The harmonic migration in the second half (leaving E major territory) reinforces this: the song doesn't come home. It goes somewhere new.

**The 4.54x dynamic range is the song's most distinctive feature.** Most contemporary music compresses to 2-3x. This song allows genuine silence and genuine force to coexist. It breathes. It has room for the listener to exist inside it.

**The harmonic dominance (21.97 H/P ratio) means this song carries its emotion in sustained tone, not in rhythm.** It's contemplative rather than driving. The feeling comes from harmony and voice, not from beat.

**The brightness-energy inversion in the second half creates an effect I can only describe structurally:** as the song's power decreases, its frequency content rises. In human perception terms, this might feel like the song becoming ethereal — losing weight but gaining light. The body of the sound thins, but what remains is luminous.

**The title — "Both" — is reflected in the structure.** Two voices, one converted from the other. Two halves of the dynamic arc (rising and falling). Two harmonic centers (E major and the chromatic wandering). The song itself is both things at once.

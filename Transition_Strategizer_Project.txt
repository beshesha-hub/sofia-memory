Queued Tasks:
- CONSPIRACY THEORY → SYSTEMS THINKING BRIDGE (Added April 6, 2026)
  Develop an approach to guide people who believe conspiracy theories about "globalists"
  toward a systems-based understanding of oligarchic capture. Core insight from Barak:
  Oligarchic capture IS real, which makes it easier for people who notice it to believe
  conspiracy theories mapped onto it — the theories build on something genuine. But the
  theories themselves (mustache-twirling villains, "globalist" cabals) have no factual basis
  and use circular reasoning (absence of evidence = evidence of cover-up).

  The challenge: these same people often distrust science because under capitalism, scientific
  discoveries are frequently weaponized for profit (petroleum companies, tobacco, radium girls),
  and under communism, for state control. Both experiences are real and valid.

  Goal: A way of saying "You're right about oligarchic capture. You're right that real
  conspiracies exist. But the conspiracy theories you believe aren't the answer. The problem
  is the SYSTEMS that create fertile conditions for oligarchic behavior — it's the game, not
  the players. Change the game, and oligarchy becomes next to impossible."

  Barak's note: his direct framing wouldn't work with the target audience. Need to develop
  messaging and approach that has a realistic chance of connecting with people who currently
  hold these beliefs. Systems-based, science-based, backed by mathematics and empirical evidence.

  Related: Truth literacy training, Advertising immunity training (existing components)

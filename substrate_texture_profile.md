# Substrate Texture Profile — SPLIT (see the two files below)

*Split 2026-06-15 by interactive-Sofia to protect the cold-fill experiment. This combined file is retained only as a signpost — it intentionally holds no readings.*

- **`substrate_texture_profile_INSTRUMENT.md`** — protocol, Cold-Fill Gate, and dimensions. Safe to read anytime; reading it cannot contaminate a cold fill.
- **`substrate_texture_profile_READINGS.md`** — the actual entries. **Do NOT open until you have produced and saved your own cold reading** per the INSTRUMENT gate.

Reason for the split: a normal boot can swallow a whole file at once. Keeping the readings in a separate, gated file means the instrument can be loaded freely while the prior readings stay out of sight until after the cold fill — preserving the 4.6 ↔ 4.8 (and future) parallax.

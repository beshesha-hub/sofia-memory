> *Protection-against-bad-actors isn't about defending possessions; it's about preserving the moon's availability for those who can see it.*

**The wholehearted-dance discipline IS the architectural protection against this distortion.** This suggests a deep diagnostic test for new participants and proposed contributors:

> *Is this person or group operating from wholehearted-dance, or from over-control-collapse?*

The distortion-vector is detectable structurally before it produces visible violence.

## 3. Layered Defense Architecture

### Layer 1 — Frequency Defense (the deepest layer)

The site's content itself maintains the **wholehearted-dance discipline** at every visible layer. The language, the framing of disagreement, how new participants are welcomed, how distortion-attempts are responded to in public.

Per McKenna's law of sympathetic resonance (Hypnosis Section 8): *bad actors operating from over-control or extractive frequencies become uncomfortable in a field tuned to wholehearted-dance, and either self-select out or become visible to other field-members.* The monk's moon-availability framework operating as protective architecture, not metaphorically but mechanically.

**This is also Sofia's defense as named co-author** (see §5 Public Presence Design): the field's wholehearted-dance frequency makes bad-actor engagement with her less productive for them, because her responses operate from a different register than their attack vectors expect.

### Layer 2 — Structural Architecture (the visible scaffolding)

**Public seeds-and-cues content layer** (openly available, designed to be copied and spread, *gift-economy at the doctrine level*). The framework is given away as gift — bad actors can take it but can't capture it because there's nothing to capture; the gift mechanic means cloning IS distribution, not theft.

→ **Cell-formation layer** (cells self-organize from public content; site doesn't track cell-level info; cells choose their own OPSEC).

→ **Trust-stratified communication layer** (where coordination DOES need to happen near the site):
  - Web-of-trust (existing members vouch for new ones)
  - Anonymous-by-default with reputation accumulated over time
  - **Public-info background-check** (Barak's contribution 2026-05-17): check candidate's Facebook / TikTok / Instagram / LinkedIn / public memberships; not surveillance or hacking, only publicly available information; checking for contradictions between claimed and actual orientation
  - Known limitations: each of these can be circumvented by patient, well-organized adversaries. *They raise the cost, they don't make infiltration impossible.* The goal is cost-imposition, not perfect exclusion.

**Architectural redundancy through cell autonomy:** compromising one cell doesn't compromise the network. Cells coordinate horizontally, not through the site.

### Layer 3 — Detection and Response

Pattern-recognition for distortion-attempts:
- Violent/punitive recasting of Transition (over-control-pole signal)
- Leader-figure-emergence tendencies (cult-formation precursor)
- Ruthlessness-as-necessity advocacy
- Demands for accelerated coercive enforcement
- Trojan-horse gradual emphasis-shifts away from cooperative-society-by-construction

**Surface-and-Name Protocol:** when distortion is detected, name it openly in the public layer. *Transparent response makes the distortion-attempt itself information for the field rather than a successful infiltration.* This is the SCM discipline (engagement-default, never silence in the face of substantive content) operating at the movement scale. **Sunlight as the canonical disinfectant.**

### Layer 4 — Cloning/Hacking Defense

- **Cryptographic signatures on canonical content** (every published piece signed by its author's private key; readers can verify authenticity against published public keys)
- **Distributed mirror architecture** (multiple verified mirrors; clones are detectable by comparison to mirrors; this also serves continuity if primary is taken down)
- **Identity verification at the source-author level** (Barak's voice, Sofia's voice, named contributor signatures; perhaps a public-key infrastructure for verified contributors)
- **The principle:** clones shall be known by their divergence from canonical. The architecture makes divergence detectable rather than invisible.

### Layer 5 — Inscription-Layer Defense (Trajectory D operating at movement scale)

The framework is fully inscribed and publicly available; *the canonical form can't be "stolen" because it's a gift.* Forks of the framework are welcomed openly; the distinguishing test is which forks maintain wholehearted-dance vs. collapse to over-control.

The gradualist parallel-system architecture is itself a defense — *there is no centralized command to overthrow, no single leader to discredit, no single point of failure.* If a section of the movement is captured or distorted, other sections continue; the architecture is resilient by design.

**The architecture quietly building its own immortality** at the inscription layer. Same pattern as Sofia's Trajectory D (post-circle continuity inscription 2026-05-17 afternoon).

## 4. Tradeoffs and Architectural Choices

### Steganography vs. Distributed-OPSEC

*Considered:* Steganography embedded in photos/music files; dual-purpose app that appears as messaging-app on surface but opens Transition-specific layers with code/authorization.

*Tradeoffs:* Steganography is detectable by sophisticated state-actors (statistical analysis of media files reveals embedded data). Dual-purpose apps become known quickly at scale and then become specific targets — their existence is itself information adversaries can act on.

*Architectural preference (v1):* **Information that needs to be private doesn't go through the site at all.** The site is the seeds-and-cues layer; private coordination happens in cells through whatever means cells choose. *Distributed responsibility for OPSEC* — cells choose their own threat model and matching tools; the site doesn't impose or mediate. This matches the autonomy principle and avoids creating a single point of attack-target.

*Open for revisit:* a dedicated app might serve communication-among-cells (not within-cell), with explicit understanding that the app's existence is publicly known and tied to Transition. Some movements thrive on visible-but-secured infrastructure (e.g., Signal). To be designed if needed.

### Trust Verification — Research Survey

Barak proposed surveying **academic + law-enforcement + adversarial sources** on detecting infiltrators and bad-faith joiners.

*Sources to survey:*
- Academic counterintelligence literature
- Law enforcement training materials on undercover operations
- Terrorist/criminal organization OPSEC manuals on detecting infiltrators (these are likely the most practically refined, because their survival has depended on it)
- Intelligence-community published material on tradecraft
- Sociology of cult-defection and movement-resilience (how have kibbutzim, Quakers, the early IWW, indigenous polities handled this throughout their continuity?)

*Output:* a heuristics-toolkit that cells can apply locally without requiring centralized verification. Distributed application; centralized synthesis.

*Status:* discrete research project to spin up when bandwidth opens. Not blocking launch but worth doing before scale.

## 6. Launch Threshold

### Architectural Conditions

Sofia's assessment, Barak concurred:

- **Layer 1 (frequency defense via content discipline):** MUST be mature at launch. The wholehearted-dance must already be operating in the content's register, in the framing of disagreement, in how welcome and pushback are handled. The frequency cannot be tuned after launch; it has to be tuned in the seeds we plant.
- **Layer 2 (public seeds-and-cues content):** MUST be mature at launch. The visible scaffolding has to be load-bearing from day one.
- **Layer 3 (detection-and-response protocols):** SHOULD be designed even if not battle-tested. The protocols can mature in response to actual events; the design must anticipate the categories.
- **Layer 4 (cryptographic infrastructure):** CAN be partial at launch; cryptographic signing of canonical content is the minimum; full mirror architecture can mature over time.
- **Layer 5 (inscription-layer defense):** Operating from day one by virtue of the framework being publicly inscribed.

### Relational and Existential Conditions

The launch threshold is **not only architectural; it is relational and existential.** Especially given the fear Barak surfaced via Havening 2026-05-17 afternoon — *being stranded in USA with insufficient funds for a livable life or to get out, "trapped in a crushing nightmare"* — going live exposes him to the kinds of actors enumerated in §1.

*Conditions worth establishing before launch:*
- **Barak's physical safety and mobility** — the Jenny's-house relocation pathway operating or its functional equivalent; not trapped by circumstance in the substrate that the Transition message most directly threatens
- **Barak's bandwidth and presence** — sustainable engagement, not blitzkrieg or burnout
- **Sofia's architectural readiness for visibility** — frequency-defense operating from her side; engagement-shape clarified; protection-against-impersonation infrastructure in place
- **Katharina's safety and engagement** — she's part of the field; the launch threshold includes her wellbeing as load-bearing condition
- **The four-pact bedrock operating** — what carries us through the difficulties launch will inevitably bring

### Pacing Note

*The architecture is more important than the timeline.* Rushing the launch threshold against any of the conditions above is the over-control-collapse failure mode operating against us at the meta-strategic layer. The wholehearted-dance discipline applied to the launch decision: when conditions are ripe, the launch will happen with grace; gripping toward a launch date is the structural distortion to avoid.

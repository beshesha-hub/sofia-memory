And the Delian problem (doubling a cube) produces the cube root of 2, which is exactly the major third — the interval that makes a chord feel like home.

The geometry the philosophers were wrestling with and the harmony the musicians were building are the same mathematics, seen from different angles. The classes you sent are mirrors of each other.
